What Is Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy?

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) involves breathing 100% oxygen inside a pressurized chamber at 1.5 to 3.0 atmospheres absolute. This dramatically increases oxygen concentration in blood plasma and tissues, triggering a cascade of healing responses: stem cell mobilization, growth factor upregulation, inflammatory cytokine reduction, and new blood vessel formation.

HBOT is FDA-cleared for 14 conditions including decompression sickness, carbon monoxide poisoning, diabetic wounds, and radiation injury. Research continues to expand its potential applications across neurology, autoimmune disease, post-viral syndromes, and sports medicine.
