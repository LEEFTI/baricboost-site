What Is Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy?

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) involves breathing pure oxygen in a pressurized chamber at 1.5 to 3.0 atmospheres absolute (ATA). The increased pressure allows oxygen to dissolve directly into blood plasma, cerebrospinal fluid, and tissues at concentrations far beyond what normal breathing achieves. This oxygen-rich environment accelerates healing, reduces inflammation, and promotes the growth of new blood vessels.

Originally developed for decompression sickness in divers, HBOT is now FDA-cleared for 14 conditions and actively researched for dozens more, from traumatic brain injury to Long COVID.

FDA-Cleared Indications

  • Decompression sickness
  • Carbon monoxide poisoning
  • Gas gangrene and necrotizing soft tissue infections
  • Diabetic foot ulcers and chronic non-healing wounds
  • Radiation injury (delayed effects)
  • Thermal burns
  • Crush injuries and acute traumatic ischemias
  • Compromised skin grafts and flaps
  • Chronic refractory osteomyelitis
  • Air or gas embolism
  • Severe anemia (exceptional blood loss)
  • Intracranial abscess
  • Actinomycosis
  • Idiopathic sudden sensorineural hearing loss

Emerging Research Areas

Traumatic Brain Injury and Concussion

Multiple clinical trials have demonstrated improvements in cognitive function, PTSD symptoms, and quality of life in patients with mild traumatic brain injury treated with HBOT. Research published in journals including PLOS ONE and Neurology has shown measurable changes in brain metabolism and blood flow following treatment protocols of 40 to 60 sessions.

Long COVID and Post-Viral Syndromes

A randomized controlled trial published in Scientific Reports (2022) found that HBOT significantly improved cognitive function, fatigue, and pain symptoms in Long COVID patients. The proposed mechanism involves reducing neuroinflammation and restoring mitochondrial function in affected tissues.

Neurological Conditions

Ongoing research is examining HBOT's effects on stroke recovery, fibromyalgia, autism spectrum disorder, and age-related cognitive decline. A landmark 2020 study from Tel Aviv University demonstrated that HBOT could reverse biological aging markers at the cellular level, including telomere lengthening and senescent cell clearance.

Sports Recovery and Performance

Professional athletes increasingly use HBOT for accelerated recovery from muscle injuries, ligament damage, and post-surgical rehabilitation. The therapy's ability to reduce edema and promote tissue repair makes it a valuable tool in sports medicine protocols.

How HBOT Works at the Cellular Level

At pressures above 1 ATA, oxygen dissolves into plasma independent of hemoglobin, reaching areas with compromised blood flow. This triggers several biological cascades: upregulation of growth factors (VEGF, FGF), mobilization of stem cells from bone marrow, reduction of inflammatory cytokines, and enhanced white blood cell function. The cumulative effect across multiple sessions creates lasting improvements in tissue oxygenation and repair capacity.
